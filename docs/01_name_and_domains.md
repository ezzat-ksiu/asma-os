## 1. Decision

**Working master brand: FOLIAVERE** — pronounced *FOH-lee-ah-VAIR* in English and **فولياڤير** in Arabic (proposed transliteration; use **فولياڤير** consistently in Arabic copy). Brand line: **“Stories, kept in a form worth keeping.”** Arabic: **«حكايات تُصاغ لتبقى.»**

This is a creative and strategic selection, not a legal clearance. Exact-name web search did not surface an obvious established exact-match publishing house in the initial search set. That is weak negative evidence only. It does not establish domain availability, absence of unregistered rights, or registrability in any territory. Before committing, an adult/authorised contracting representative must commission a professional clearance search and make any filing lawfully.

## 2. AUREVIUM: why we reject it

An EU trademark record identified as application/registration **019182327 — AUREVIUM**, recorded as registered on 29 October 2025 in the visible third-party record. Its displayed classes are not the core publishing classes, so this is not proof that a filing in Classes 9, 16, 41 or 42 would automatically be refused. It is enough to reject the claim that the name is “clear” or “the rarest globally”. An existing web presence at `aurevium.net` adds search and identity ambiguity. The Board rejects AUREVIUM as the launch name pending a full clearance opinion.

Reference: [AUREVIUM record — third-party trademark index](https://www.trademarkelite.com/europe/trademark/trademark-detail/019182327/Aurevium). Confirm status directly in [EUIPO search](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip) and [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/).

### Clearance work required

Search exact and similar forms, transliterations, stems, phonetics and logos for: `FOLIAVERE`, `FOLIA VERE`, `FOLIAVÉRE`, `FOLIAVERA`, `FOLIAVERO`, `FOLIAVERE PRESS`, `FOLIAVERE EDITIONS`, `فولياڤير`, `فوليا فير`. Search Classes 9 (downloadable publications/software), 16 (printed matter), 41 (publishing/education/entertainment) and 42 (software/SaaS/design/technology services). Also inspect Classes 35 (retail/marketing), 40 (printing), and 45 only if the business model expands into relevant legal or personal services. Nice classes are not a magic shield: related goods/services and local law matter.

Official search routes: [USPTO trademark search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching), [EUIPO](https://www.euipo.europa.eu/en/search-ip), [WIPO](https://branddb.wipo.int/). Domain registrations can be checked through registrar search and RDAP; [ICANN RDAP update](https://www.icann.org/en/announcements/details/icann-update-launching-rdap-sunsetting-whois-27-01-2025-en).

Purchase only the primary `.com` initially if available and lawfully registrable. Do not buy five variants before clearance. Use the exact domain in invoices and customer emails only after registration and DNS verification. Enable registrar lock, auto-renewal, multi-factor authentication and separate recovery credentials. Use a privacy-preserving registration service only where local rules permit and where the true legal registrant is accurately disclosed to the registrar.

## 6. Social handle policy

Target handle format: `@foliavere` on Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube and LinkedIn. If taken, prefer one consistent suffix such as `@foliavere.editions`. Do not impersonate an existing account, buy suspicious handles, or use misleading location suffixes. Reserve only the platforms that can be actively maintained. The first social identity should show synthetic portfolio work, production process, typography and paper samples—not customer/child likenesses.

## 7. Arabic naming rules

- Never alternate between فوليافير / فولياڤير / فوليا فير in one publication.
- The English legal name, once lawfully registered, must remain exact in contracts and invoices.
- Test search behaviour for both the Arabic transliteration and Latin master name before building Arabic SEO routes.
