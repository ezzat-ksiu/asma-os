### Editorial notes for AV-001

- Keep sentences short and readable aloud; do not exceed the target word density in the actual typeset proof.
- The character Noor is a wholly fictional illustration. Do not base her on any uploaded child photo.
- All text is typeset after illustration generation. Never ask an image model to render Arabic typography inside the illustration.
- Review text accuracy, page continuity, cultural nuance and trim safety before producing a physical sample.

## 3. Remaining five sample creative directions

### AV-002 — The Map of Small Wonders

A 32-page English story about Theo, a fictional child who notices ordinary neighbourhood details others overlook. Each page pairs one observation with a question. Illustration palette: ivory, forest, dusty blue and restrained terracotta. Learning prompt: “What did you notice when you slowed down?” Avoid claiming improved attention or intelligence.

### AV-003 — Le Jardin des Histoires

A 32-page French story about Lina and neighbours transforming a neglected courtyard into a shared story garden. The plot values cooperation, listening and plant care. Use simple French sentences, accurate accents and no real school or location names. Artwork should show a fictional courtyard with a clear path and safe accessible planting beds.

### AV-004 — The Rooms We Carry

An adult illustrated novella sample, 32-page extract rather than a full-length memoir. A fictional narrator revisits a kitchen, a train compartment, a family table and a quiet reading room. Typography leads; illustrations are restrained full-bleed vignettes. Any customer commissioned work with 60–120 pages requires a separate BookSpec page-count extension and distinct economic quote.

### AV-005 — The Art of Welcome

A fictional luxury-hospitality coffee-table sample: architecture, materials, service rituals, local craft and guest care. No real hotel logo, actual guest or fabricated testimonial. B2B buyer receives a content-rights register, brand approval stages and a print proof.

### AV-006 — بين ضفتين

A bilingual family sample about a fictional household using Arabic and English across meals, sayings, objects and family celebrations. Design alternating page languages with a bilingual glossary. Test mixed-script punctuation, numerals, line breaks and RTL/LTR reading order in final PDF.

## 4. Unboxing journey

**Objective:** quiet anticipation and confident product protection, not emotional manipulation. Base packaging target is USD 5.00 per order as an estimate. The current CostInputs model uses USD 5.00; this is a planning allowance, not a vendor quote.

### Reveal order

1. Outer shipper with no personal name or child details visible externally.
2. Paper wrap and discreet seal.
3. Book front cover revealed first.
4. Certificate and letter placed inside the front cover or included as separate insert.
5. QR card with a clear explanation of what the link opens and how long it remains available.
6. Optional audio play control; never auto-play.

### Optional premium gift box

The base product economics do **not** include a custom rigid gift box, foil-stamped lid, magnetic closure or bespoke ribbon. Those require a new live supplier quote. Add a separate premium packaging charge only after sample testing, dimensional shipping cost, damage risk and contribution margin have been recalculated. Do not absorb an unquoted $15–$30 gift box into the $5 packaging allowance.

## 5. QR, audio and vault safety

- QR resolves to a random high-entropy reference or an authenticated vault route; it must not contain customer email, child name or predictable database ID.
- Public vault page reveals only serial, issue status and approved redacted metadata.
- Audio files contain only the approved final manuscript and licensed voice output.
- If a customer deletes their account or the audio is no longer authorised, revoke the link and delete the file according to the published retention policy.
- Provide a text transcript and accessible controls; QR is an access shortcut, not the only way to access important content.
